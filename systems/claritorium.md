# Claritorium

**Cultivate clarity.**

Claritorium is the broader System for cultivating coherence between what we believe, how we live, and what we learn. It treats clarity as something grown through the interaction of structure, flow, and environment.

## Core Philosophy

**Believe → Live → Learn ↻**

- **Believe — Direction over Destination.** Establish a meaningful heading without pretending the destination can be perfectly known.
- **Live — Embodiment over Accumulation.** Put what you believe into contact with present reality.
- **Learn — Reality over Representation.** Integrate what reality teaches and let it refine belief and action.

A temporal lens complements the philosophy:

- **Believe → Future**
- **Live → Present**
- **Learn → Past**

The future needs Direction. The present needs Presence. The past needs Integration.

## Living Movements

**Direction → Reflection → Expression**

- **Direction** — Choose what matters.
- **Reflection** — Learn from experience.
- **Expression** — Live what you believe.

These describe movement through the Claritorium.

## The Three Models

### Clarity Codex

**Structure**

**Define what guides.**

Clarity Codex is the structural condition of clarity. It gives form to purpose, values, principles, Models, Patterns, shared language, and other durable representations.

**Clarity Codex → Direction**

### Clarity Current

**Flow**

**Integrate what emerges.**

Clarity Current is the flowing condition of clarity. It describes how attention, information, experience, feedback, and meaning move and connect.

**Clarity Current → Reflection**

### Clarity Climate

**Environment**

**Shape what becomes possible.**

Clarity Climate is the environmental condition of clarity. Culture, constraints, relationships, Signals, spaces, incentives, expectations, and repeated conditions influence what can emerge and endure.

**Clarity Climate → Expression**

The Models describe conditions. Direction, Reflection, and Expression describe movement through those conditions. The correspondence is strong but not exclusive: every condition can influence every movement.

## Core Formula

**Claritorium = Hidden Structure × Invisible Force × Emergent Environment**

The original ecology makes the relationship tangible:

- **Codex = Soil & Roots**
- **Current = Water & Wind**
- **Climate = Sunlight & Atmosphere**

The metaphor serves cultivation: structure gives clarity form, flow keeps it moving, and environment shapes what can flourish.

**Coherence is cultivated, not completed.**
