# Solarium

**Expand perspective.**

Solarium is the perspective System. It primarily strengthens Reflection by expanding the frame through which something is understood.

## Persistent Perspectives

Solarium uses a persistent set of chosen figures as perspective lenses. Choose people who embody distinct values, philosophies, worldviews, ways of seeing, forms of judgment, or qualities worth internalizing.

The persistence matters. Instead of assembling a new panel of generic experts for every question, return to a stable set of distinct perspectives. Over time, those lenses can become internalized ways of seeing.

Real people can also contribute lived experience, relationship, context, surprise, and genuine disagreement. The aim is not to maximize voices, but to introduce the right difference.

## Core Movement

**Engage → Contrast → Integrate**

### Engage

Let the chosen perspectives encounter the question independently.

### Contrast

Preserve meaningful differences long enough to reveal assumptions, values, priorities, tradeoffs, competing definitions of the problem, and different ways of seeing. Do not force premature consensus.

### Integrate

Use what the perspectives reveal to improve your own judgment. Do not outsource the conclusion.

## The Three Models

### Perspective Council

Engage the chosen persistent perspectives. Seek meaningful difference rather than prestige, agreement, or validation.

### Constructive Tension

Protect useful disagreement long enough to learn from it. Compare assumptions, values, tradeoffs, and second-order effects before converging.

### Integrated Judgment

Integrate what emerges while retaining responsibility for the final judgment. Synthesis should improve judgment, not replace it.

> **What can I see now that I could not see from my original frame?**

Solarium is not a consensus engine, an answer oracle, generic expert aggregation, or a system for outsourcing judgment. Its purpose is to expand perspective.
