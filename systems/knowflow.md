# KnowFlow

**Develop understanding.**

KnowFlow is the learning System. It keeps understanding answerable to reality and operates across Direction, Reflection, and Expression rather than belonging to Reflection alone.

## Core Flywheel

**Notice → Question → Test → Understand → Express ↻**

### Notice

Capture a meaningful Signal: an observation, tension, surprise, or curiosity.

### Question

Turn the Signal into an Inquiry worth exploring.

### Test

Put the idea into contact with evidence, experience, observation, or reality.

### Understand

Distill what reality revealed into durable understanding.

### Express

Make understanding visible through articulation or embodiment.

KnowFlow is a flywheel, not a pipeline. Expression creates experience, and experience creates new Signals.

## The Three Models

### Inquiry Formation

**Turn attention into questions.**

Inquiry Formation primarily covers **Notice → Question**. It separates what merely caught attention from what deserves exploration, then shapes an open but testable Inquiry.

### Reality Testing

**Put ideas against reality.**

Reality Testing primarily covers **Test**. It makes a belief explicit, seeks contact that could challenge it, and updates the Working Theory when reality reveals something different.

### Knowledge Integration

**Make learning durable.**

Knowledge Integration primarily covers **Understand → Express**. It distills experience, connects new understanding to existing Patterns, Models, and Systems, and gives the learning form through articulation or embodiment.

Recognition, distillation, compression, synthesis, and connection are mechanisms within KnowFlow. Together they help Knowledge develop from **Pattern → Model → System** without treating understanding as finished.

**Reality > Representation**
