# Praxis

**Embody understanding.**

Praxis is the embodiment System. It operates primarily within Expression while remaining informed by Direction and Reflection.

Praxis asks:

> **How should what I understand change how I live?**

## Core Movement

**Iteration → Embodiment → Persistence**

In plain language: **Try → Embody → Sustain**

## The Three Models

### Adaptive Experimentation

**Learn through action.**

Use a bounded test when the right Practice is uncertain. A good Experiment:

- makes an assumption explicit
- creates real contact with reality
- is small enough to run
- produces observable experience
- can change the Working Theory
- ends or evolves when enough has been learned

### Embodied Practice

**Live what you believe.**

Practice is sustained embodiment of something worth living. It translates principles into choices, understanding into behavior, values into action, and beliefs into lived experience.

Judge Practice by coherence and transformation rather than accumulation or streaks.

### Sustainable Rhythm

**Sustain what matters.**

Rhythms create recurring conditions that make meaningful Practice easier to return to. Favor consistency over intensity, cadence over force, adaptation over rigidity, and persistence over short-term optimization.

## Experiment and Practice

**Experiment = a bounded test used to learn.**

**Practice = sustained embodiment of something worth living.**

Experiments generate learning. Practice generates transformation. Experiments help discover what is worth practicing; Rhythm helps worthwhile Practice endure.

Praxis does not contain the entire Direction → Reflection → Expression architecture. That broader architecture belongs to Claritorium.
