# Experiment

**Experiment = a bounded test used to learn.**

## Assertion

What do I currently believe?

## Test

What am I going to try?

The test should be bounded and capable of changing the assertion.

## Observation

What actually happened?

## Learning

What did I learn?

What changed in my understanding?
